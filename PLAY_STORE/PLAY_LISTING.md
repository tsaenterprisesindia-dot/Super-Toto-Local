## Full description

> **Note:** This is an **internal / closed test build**. The app connects to a local
> development backend (`127.0.0.1:5000`) and is intended for testing only — not for
> public production use. Register as a Rider to book a toto, auto or taxi; register as
> a Driver to accept and fulfil trips.

Super Toto Local connects riders with local e-rickshaw (toto), auto-rickshaw and taxi drivers.

**For Riders**
- Book a toto, auto or taxi in a few taps
- Choose your vehicle and, for shared trips, book just the seats you need
- See transparent, itemised fares (base, distance, time, surge, GST)
- Share / track your live trip with the driver
- Pay online and get a GST invoice
- Rate and review your driver

**For Drivers**
- Simple document-based KYC and police-verification onboarding
- GPS-based live tracking
- Accept trips, see earnings, and manage your documents and vehicle
- Multilingual interface (English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil)

**Safety & governance**
- Driver verification: Aadhaar, driving licence, police clearance and vehicle documents
- GPS tracking + live trip sharing
- Surge-priced but state-surge-capped fares
- Grievance officer and mandatory disclosures as required by law

Operating matters, GST compliance and mandatory disclosures are published under the
Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, the Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines,
and the IT Rules, 2021.
