## Block 1 — Hero (Text box)

🛺 **Super Toto Local**

Ride-hailing for local totos & e-rickshaws — book a toto, track your driver live,
and pay by UPI. A full-stack demo built with React, Node.js, MongoDB and Socket.io,
with **Face Recognition login** and support for **six Indian languages**.

## Block 2 — What you get (Text box, use bullets)

- 👤 **Rider** — pick pickup/drop on a live map, instant fare estimate, request a toto,
  track the driver in real time, cancel, pay via UPI, rate the driver, ride history
- 🛺 **Driver** — online/offline toggle, accept ride requests, run the trip, track earnings
- 🛠️ **Admin** — live stats, approve/block drivers, view all rides, set state-wise fare policies
- 😀 **Face Recognition login** — riders & drivers scan their face to log in (password always works as fallback)
- 🔌 **Live tracking** — real-time GPS streaming with nearest-driver dispatch
- 🧾 **GoI-ready compliance** — state-wise notified fare framework, fare transparency pages
